## icon

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a square premium mobile app icon for a cozy watercolor virtual cat home game. A very cute round calico kitten face fills the center, white fur, orange patch over its right ear and forehead, charcoal patch over left ear, warm dark brown clean thick outlines, small shiny dark eyes and gentle tiny smiling w mouth. Rounded soft cheeks, tiny peach inner ears. Behind the kitten a simple sage green little house arch and warm cream background with a subtle peach glow. Hand painted soft storybook watercolor, simplified clear silhouette readable at 48 pixels, no glossy 3D, no clutter. Full bleed opaque square background, NO pre-rounded app icon corners, NO text, NO letters, NO words, NO watermark, no egg-shaped device. Cat and ears completely inside central 76 percent safe area. Calm inviting premium friendly image, consistent with a warm cream peach sage virtual pet game.

Dosya: `sprite_preview/patilife_v2/assets/brand/patilife-icon.png`

## launch

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE transparent sprite sheet with FOUR equally spaced square cells in a single horizontal row. Same simple chibi calico kitten, perfectly consistent size, silhouette, position, line weights and fur patches in all four cells. Full body sitting beside a small peach yarn ball. White fur, orange patch right forehead, charcoal patch left forehead, warm dark brown thick neat outlines and minimal soft watercolor color. Frame1 kitten eyes open, paw down. Frame2 eyelids half closed and right paw lifting slightly. Frame3 eyes closed smiling with paw raised waving. Frame4 eyes open smiling with paw lowering. Tiny expression change, no body translation. Entire cat plus yarn inside central 70 percent of EACH square cell with wide transparent gutters and no overlaps. Actual transparent background. No text, no labels, no borders, no grid lines, no numerals. Intended for a cozy mobile game launch animation. Clear readable cartoon shapes, no realistic fur details, no 3D.

Dosya: `sprite_preview/patilife_v2/assets/brand/patilife-launch.png`
