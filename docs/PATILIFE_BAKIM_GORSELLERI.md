## Sarman bakım atlası

Dosya: `sarman-kitten-care.png`. 4 sütun × 3 satır. Yeme, içme ve uyuma döngüleri.

Production transparent ACTION SPRITE ATLAS for the exact same simple PatiLife orange kitten in the reference. Reference is character identity and cartoon style, NOT the output layout. Make a NEW 4 columns by 3 rows grid, EXACTLY TWELVE equally spaced square cells. Whole character entirely inside each cell, 12% transparent margins, common floor baseline at 84% cell height, same size all cells. Simple flat cream and orange shapes, warm brown outlines, dot eyes and tiny w mouth. No fur detail or 3D. Row 1 four sequential EATING frames: kitten on four paws, right-facing side/three-quarter view, head lowered to ground level just ahead of front paws; small distinct head dips and chewing mouth open/closed, tail relaxed. Row 2 four sequential DRINKING frames: same right-facing lowered pose, tiny pink tongue out then in and subtle head dips, readable lapping cycle. Row 3 four sequential SLEEPING frames: kitten curled into a round loaf on ground, eyes closed, tail curled against body, slight breathing rise/fall. Preserve head patch, cream muzzle and orange striped tail across all frames. DO NOT include bowls, food, beds, water, ground, scenery, shadows, labels, cell borders, text or symbols; game will draw props separately. No extra rows, no repeated standing poses; poses must be clear eat/drink/sleep. Genuine transparent alpha, no background. Landscape 4:3 canvas ideal 1536x1152. Match reference's very simple cute 2D cartoon style precisely.
